About

I’m Jiri. I studied computer science at PXL in Hasselt and graduated cum laude in 2014. I started out building websites at an agency, spent five years turning sensor data into apps people could use, led mobile at a start-up and grew into a Staff role at Shopify. The part I enjoy most is the unglamorous part: making a laggy screen feel instant, making flaky tests boring, and writing the design doc that lets a team move without me. I spend a good share of my week mentoring, and lately a lot of it on AI-assisted workflows with Claude Code and custom MCP tools. Write to me.
